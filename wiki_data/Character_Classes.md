# Character Classes

Duke County features distinct character backgrounds that influence starting skills, equipment, and playstyles in the harsh 1840s frontier.

## Starting Backgrounds

### Gunslinger
- **Primary Role**: Combat Specialist, Quick Draw
- **Starting Equipment**: Colt Paterson Revolver, Light Leather Vest
- **Key Skills**: Marksmanship, Quick Reflexes
- **Starting Location**: Dusty saloon in frontier town

**Core Abilities:**
- **Quick Draw**: Lightning-fast weapon draw for first shot advantage
- **Fan the Hammer**: Rapid fire multiple rounds with reduced accuracy
- **Steady Aim**: Increased accuracy when standing still
- **Combat Roll**: Dodge incoming attacks with a quick roll

### Trapper
- **Primary Role**: Hunter, Tracker, Survival Expert
- **Starting Equipment**: Hunting Rifle, Skinning Knife, Fur Coat
- **Key Skills**: Hunting, Tracking, Crafting
- **Starting Location**: Remote cabin in the wilderness

**Core Abilities:**
- **Track Animals**: Follow animal trails to find hunting grounds
- **Set Traps**: Place bear traps and snares for game
- **Expert Skinner**: Get bonus materials from hunted animals
- **Wilderness Survival**: Increased resistance to cold and hunger

### Prospector
- **Primary Role**: Resource Gatherer, Craftsman
- **Starting Equipment**: Pickaxe, Pan, Work Clothes
- **Key Skills**: Mining, Resource Detection, Metalworking
- **Starting Location**: Mining camp near rocky terrain

**Core Abilities:**
- **Ore Sense**: Detect valuable mineral deposits nearby
- **Expert Miner**: Gather resources faster and with better yields
- **Basic Smithing**: Craft and repair metal tools and weapons
- **Strong Back**: Carry more weight in inventory

### Outlaw
- **Primary Role**: Raider, Thief, PvP Specialist
- **Starting Equipment**: Sawed-off Shotgun, Bandana Mask, Dark Duster
- **Key Skills**: Stealth, Looting, Intimidation
- **Starting Location**: Hidden outlaw camp

**Core Abilities:**
- **Ambush**: Deal increased damage from stealth
- **Intimidate**: Force NPCs to surrender valuables
- **Lock Picking**: Open locked containers and doors
- **Covered Escape**: Smoke bomb for quick getaway

## Advanced Paths (Unlocked through gameplay)

- **Marshal** (Gunslinger path with law enforcement focus)
- **Mountain Man** (Trapper specialized in extreme survival)
- **Ranch Owner** (Prospector focused on settlement building)
- **Gang Leader** (Outlaw with leadership abilities)

## Character Progression

- Skill-based system: improve through use
- No traditional levels - skills increase with practice
- Choose specializations as you develop your character
- Multiple viable playstyles for each background
