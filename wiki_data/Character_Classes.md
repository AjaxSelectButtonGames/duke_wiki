# Character Classes

Duke County features a diverse set of character classes, each with unique abilities, playstyles, and progression paths.

## Core Classes

### Warrior
- **Primary Role**: Tank, Melee DPS
- **Weapon Proficiency**: Swords, Axes, Maces, Shields
- **Armor Type**: Heavy (Plate)
- **Key Attributes**: Strength, Constitution

**Abilities:**
- **Shield Bash**: Stuns enemy for 2 seconds
- **Battle Cry**: Increases party attack by 15% for 10 seconds
- **Charge**: Dashes to target, dealing damage on impact
- **Last Stand**: Reduces damage taken by 50% when below 25% HP

### Mage
- **Primary Role**: Ranged DPS, Crowd Control
- **Weapon Proficiency**: Staffs, Wands
- **Armor Type**: Light (Cloth)
- **Key Attributes**: Intelligence, Willpower

**Abilities:**
- **Fireball**: High damage single-target spell
- **Ice Nova**: AOE freeze effect
- **Arcane Shield**: Absorbs incoming damage
- **Teleport**: Instant movement to target location

### Rogue
- **Primary Role**: Melee DPS, Stealth
- **Weapon Proficiency**: Daggers, Short Swords, Bows
- **Armor Type**: Medium (Leather)
- **Key Attributes**: Dexterity, Agility

**Abilities:**
- **Backstab**: Massive damage from behind
- **Stealth**: Become invisible for 10 seconds
- **Poison Strike**: DoT effect over 15 seconds
- **Evasion**: 50% chance to dodge attacks for 5 seconds

### Cleric
- **Primary Role**: Healer, Support
- **Weapon Proficiency**: Maces, Staves, Shields
- **Armor Type**: Medium (Chain)
- **Key Attributes**: Wisdom, Spirit

**Abilities:**
- **Healing Light**: Restore ally HP
- **Divine Protection**: Damage immunity shield
- **Resurrection**: Revive fallen party member
- **Holy Smite**: Ranged damage spell

## Advanced Classes (Unlocked at Level 50)

- **Paladin** (Warrior + Cleric)
- **Battle Mage** (Warrior + Mage)
- **Assassin** (Rogue + Mage)
- **Shadow Priest** (Cleric + Rogue)

## Class Balance Considerations

- Each class should be viable in both PvE and PvP
- No class should be strictly better than another
- Encourage class diversity in party composition
- Regular balance patches based on player feedback
