# Combat Mechanics

The combat system in Duke County is designed to be engaging, skill-based, and rewarding for players who master their class.

## Core Concepts

### Action Combat
- Real-time combat with manual targeting
- No auto-attacks (all abilities are activated)
- Movement during combat is crucial
- Positioning matters for abilities and dodging

### Resource Systems

Each class uses a unique resource:

| Class | Resource | Regeneration |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| Warrior | Rage | Generated by dealing/taking damage |
| Mage | Mana | Regenerates over time |
| Rogue | Energy | Fast regeneration |
| Cleric | Holy Power | Generated by casting spells |

### Combat Stats

**Primary Stats:**
- Strength: Increases physical damage
- Dexterity: Increases critical chance and dodge
- Intelligence: Increases spell power
- Constitution: Increases health pool
- Wisdom: Increases mana pool
- Spirit: Increases resource regeneration

**Secondary Stats:**
- Attack Power
- Spell Power
- Critical Strike Chance
- Critical Strike Damage
- Armor
- Magic Resistance
- Dodge Chance
- Block Chance

## Ability System

### Global Cooldown (GCD)
- 1.5 second cooldown between most abilities
- Some instant abilities are off-GCD
- Encourages strategic ability usage

### Ability Types

**Instant Cast**: Execute immediately
**Cast Time**: Requires channeling (can be interrupted)
**Channeled**: Continuous casting over duration
**AOE**: Area of effect abilities
**DOT**: Damage over time effects
**HOT**: Healing over time effects

### Combo System

Certain abilities chain together for bonus effects:

```
Rogue Example:
1. Ambush → 2. Eviscerate (50% bonus damage)
1. Ambush → 2. Kidney Shot (extended stun duration)
```

## Defense Mechanics

### Dodge
- Chance-based avoidance
- Scales with Dexterity
- Cannot dodge magical attacks

### Block
- Reduces damage by a percentage
- Requires shield equipped
- Works against physical and some magical attacks

### Parry
- Deflects melee attacks
- Small window for perfect parry (full damage negation)
- Skill-based timing mechanic

## Crowd Control

**Hard CC** (Full control loss):
- Stun: Cannot act
- Sleep: Broken by damage
- Fear: Run randomly
- Charm: Controlled by enemy

**Soft CC** (Partial control):
- Slow: Reduced movement speed
- Root: Cannot move but can cast
- Silence: Cannot cast spells
- Disarm: Cannot use weapon abilities

**Diminishing Returns**: Each successive CC of same type is reduced by 50%

## PvP Combat

### Balance Adjustments
- Some abilities have reduced effectiveness in PvP
- Healing reduced by 50% in PvP zones
- CC duration reduced by 30% against players

### Death and Respawn
- **PvE**: Respawn at nearest graveyard, minor durability loss
- **PvP**: Respawn at graveyard, can be looted for small amount of currency

## Boss Mechanics

### Enrage Timers
- Bosses enrage after a set time
- Increases damage output significantly
- Creates DPS check for groups

### Phase Transitions
- Multi-phase boss fights
- Abilities change per phase
- Environmental hazards

### Telegraphed Attacks
- Visual indicators for dangerous abilities
- Gives players time to react
- Encourages awareness and positioning
