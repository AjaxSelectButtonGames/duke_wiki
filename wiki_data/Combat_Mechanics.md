# Combat Mechanics

The combat system in Duke County is designed to be realistic, tense, and authentic to the 1840s Western frontier experience.

## Core Concepts

### Realistic Gunplay
- Manual aiming with iron sights
- Bullet physics with drop and travel time
- Recoil affects accuracy
- Reload times based on weapon type

### Weapon Types

**Revolvers:**
- Colt Paterson (5-shot, .36 caliber)
- Quick to draw and fire
- Moderate damage, limited range
- Reload time: 10-15 seconds

**Rifles:**
- Hawken Rifle (single-shot, .50 caliber)
- High damage, long range
- Slow to reload
- Reload time: 20-30 seconds

**Shotguns:**
- Double-barrel shotgun
- Devastating close range
- Wide spread pattern
- Reload time: 8-12 seconds

**Bows:**
- Silent hunting weapon
- Infinite ammo (craftable arrows)
- Arcing trajectory
- Good for stealth

**Melee:**
- Hunting knife, hatchet, tomahawk
- Last resort or stealth kills
- Quick attacks but risky

### Combat Stats

**Character Attributes:**
- **Shooting Accuracy**: Improves with practice
- **Steadiness**: Reduces weapon sway
- **Reload Speed**: Faster reloading with experience
- **Health**: Can be increased through survival
- **Stamina**: Affects sprinting and melee attacks

**Weapon Stats:**
- Damage
- Range
- Accuracy
- Reload Time
- Durability

## Combat Mechanics

### Aiming System
- Free-aim with crosshair
- Hold breath to steady aim (drains stamina)
- Movement reduces accuracy significantly
- Crouching improves stability

### Cover System
- Take cover behind rocks, trees, buildings
- Peek out to fire
- Cover can be destroyed by gunfire
- Use terrain strategically

### Damage System
- **Headshots**: Instant kill or critical damage
- **Body shots**: Moderate damage
- **Limb shots**: Reduced damage, can cause bleeding
- **Bleeding**: Damage over time, requires bandaging

### Healing & Recovery
- Bandages stop bleeding
- Medicine restores health slowly
- Food provides minor health regeneration
- Rest at camp for full recovery
- Death is permanent in hardcore mode

## PvP Combat

### Lawless Zones
- Open PvP in certain territories
- No penalties for killing players
- Full loot on death (partial in normal zones)

### Duels
- Challenge players to honorable duels
- Winner takes agreed stakes
- No reputation loss

### Posses
- Form groups up to 8 players
- Coordinate attacks on rival groups
- Share loot from raids

### Bounties
- Players with high outlaw reputation get bounties
- Other players can hunt them for rewards
- Bounty increases with crimes

## Wildlife Combat

### Predators
- **Bears**: Extremely dangerous, high health, powerful attacks
- **Wolves**: Hunt in packs, fast and aggressive
- **Mountain Lions**: Ambush predators, high damage

### Hunting
- Track animals using footprints and signs
- Position downwind to avoid detection
- Clean kills yield better quality pelts
- Shoot vital organs for quick kills

### Animal Behavior
- Animals flee when wounded
- Predators may charge when cornered
- Some animals more active at night

## Survival Combat

### Outlaw Encounters
- Bandits ambush on trails
- Outlaw camps can be raided
- Defend your camp from raids

### Environmental Hazards
- Rattlesnakes in desert regions
- Risk of infection from wounds
- Hypothermia in cold weather
- Heatstroke in desert

## Combat Tips

1. **Conserve Ammo**: Every shot counts
2. **Use Cover**: Don't stand in the open
3. **Aim for Vitals**: Headshots save ammunition
4. **Know When to Run**: Not every fight is worth it
5. **Maintain Weapons**: Keep guns cleaned and repaired
